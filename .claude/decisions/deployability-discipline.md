This repo follows the Bodai deployability discipline (standalone-by-default, harness-agnostic interface, serverless-deployable runtime). Canonical rule and enforcement guidance: [`oneiric/docs/adr/0001-deployability-discipline.md`](https://github.com/lesleslie/oneiric/blob/main/docs/adr/0001-deployability-discipline.md). Per-repo sign-off status is tracked in that ADR's Sign-offs table.
